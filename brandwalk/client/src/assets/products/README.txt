Drop real product photos in this folder and they replace the drawn shoes automatically.

Rules:
- File name = product slug + extension (png, webp, jpg or avif), e.g.  oxford-heritage.png
- Best look: transparent PNG/WebP cut-out, side view, shoe pointing right, about 1200px wide.
- Also supported: hero.png (big home-page shoe) and about.png (About section).
- Only use photos you own or that the brand/distributor has licensed to you.
